Self-initiated workflow

Community-Aware Comment Drafting & QA Workflow

I developed a reusable editorial QA workflow for my own work: extract context, inventory facts, check community and brand safety, choose a commentable hook, generate candidates, reject factual or AI-sounding output, and validate the 25–35 word requirement.

  1. 01Extract context
  2. 02Inventory facts
  3. 03Check community fit
  4. 04Run brand-safety gate
  5. 05Select a concrete hook
  6. 06Draft candidates
  7. 07Reject weak output
  8. 08Validate and review

This workflow supports human review. It does not post, vote, or manage accounts, and I do not claim team adoption or measured efficiency gains.

What changed in my practice

This work taught me to separate historical account assets from personally attributable outcomes, and to treat AI as editorial support rather than a substitute for community judgment.
