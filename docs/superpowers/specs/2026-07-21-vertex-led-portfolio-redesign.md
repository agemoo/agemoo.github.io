# Vertex-Led Portfolio Redesign

## Goal

Rebuild Mukun Sun's bilingual portfolio around his current Vertex Marketing
internship while preserving the site's distinctive warm, dark visual identity.
The redesigned site must help a recruiter understand within the first screen
that Mukun works at the intersection of overseas community operations,
cross-cultural content judgment, evidence, and lightweight AI workflows.

Success means that Vertex Marketing is the first and most prominent case,
project evidence appears before education and generic skill lists, every metric
has a clear attribution boundary, and the site remains credible for both North
American and Chinese overseas-marketing roles.

## Audience and Language

The primary audiences are:

1. North American recruiters for student roles and internships in social
   media, community, content marketing, and growth marketing.
2. Chinese recruiters hiring for overseas social media, community operations,
   content strategy, and growth-oriented roles.

English remains the default language. The existing visible `EN / 中文` switch
and persistent language preference remain. Both languages must communicate the
same claims and evidence boundaries rather than using a shortened Chinese or
English version.

## Positioning

Primary English positioning:

> Social media operations shaped by community context, evidence, and better
> workflows.

Supporting statement:

> I'm Mukun Sun, a Strategic Communication student currently working in
> overseas community operations at Vertex Marketing. I combine cross-cultural
> content judgment, performance analysis, and lightweight AI workflows.

The Chinese copy must express the same meaning naturally, not translate it
word for word.

Paid media remains a supporting area of study and tool familiarity. It must not
be the lead positioning until the portfolio contains a real, attributable paid
campaign case.

## Information Architecture

Use a split-studio, index-first structure.

On wide screens, a compact identity rail introduces Mukun and exposes the main
actions while the project index occupies the larger content column. On mobile,
the two areas stack without sticky positioning or horizontal overflow.

The page order is:

1. Hero and current status
2. Featured Work
   - Vertex Marketing
   - Track & Traction
   - Jazz & Event Marketing
3. Selected Experiments
4. Creative Archive
5. About and Education
6. Contact

Work must appear before education and generic capabilities. Skills should be
demonstrated inside cases; a compact tools line may summarize supporting
software near the About section.

## Hero

The hero uses approximately 65–75% of the first viewport rather than a full
viewport name treatment. It includes:

- Mukun Sun's name;
- the positioning statement;
- a short supporting statement;
- current status: Vertex Marketing internship and Southern Utah University;
- `View Vertex Case` and `Download Résumé` actions;
- the language switch in the top navigation.

The hero contains no decorative metrics, availability pulse, oversized ghost
character, marquee, or continuously animated grain. One restrained entrance
sequence may introduce the hero as a single coordinated moment.

## Featured Case 01: Vertex Marketing

### Identity

Display the company name publicly:

- Organization: Vertex Marketing
- Type: Social Media Operations Internship
- Year: 2026
- Role: Community operations, content analysis, and self-initiated workflow
  improvement

Do not imply ownership of the company's overall strategy, official brand
accounts, or team-wide systems.

### Community Operations

The case may state that Mukun:

- participated in operating five Reddit community accounts;
- participated in the early setup and Moderator administration of one branded
  subreddit;
- worked across consumer technology, smart-home, lifestyle, finance, and
  family-oriented communities;
- adapted work to subreddit rules, community context, and visible content
  performance.

The public site must not disclose account names, usernames, credentials,
internal SOPs, colleague identities, client materials, or internal links.

### Approved Data Source

The sole source of quantitative Vertex claims is:

`D:/knowledge-base/wiki/findings/vertex-internship-reddit-account-portfolio.md`

Approved figures:

- 5 representative accounts reviewed and participated in operating;
- 1 branded subreddit with Moderator access in an early setup stage;
- 15,433 cumulative Karma;
- 472 historical contributions: 226 posts and 246 comments;
- 16 representative pieces of content analyzed, 15 with visible view counts;
- 793K cumulative views across those 15 pieces;
- 3,548 cumulative upvotes and 482 comments across the 16 pieces;
- 406K peak views, 891 peak upvotes, 90 peak comments, and 100% peak upvote
  ratio on individual pieces;
- 91.7% highest observed United States audience share;
- at least 15 communities represented.

Do not derive, total, or supplement Vertex figures from internship diaries.

### Attribution Boundary

The data panel must carry a visible context note in both languages:

> Portfolio context—not solely post-hire growth. These figures describe
> representative historical assets from accounts I participated in operating
> and analyzing.

The site must distinguish:

- `Historical account portfolio`: assets already associated with the accounts;
- `My role`: operations, analysis, contextual adaptation, and workflow design;
- `Personally attributable results`: only future results supported by separate
  post-hire measurement.

The site must not call the approved account-portfolio figures results created
by Mukun after joining Vertex.

### Self-Initiated Workflow Skill

Include a subsection titled `Community-Aware Comment Drafting & QA Workflow`.
Describe it as a reusable editorial QA workflow Mukun developed for his own
work. It may describe these verified components:

1. post and subreddit context extraction;
2. a fact inventory separating stated facts, hypotheses, and unknowns;
3. community-fit and brand-safety gates;
4. selection of a post-specific commentable hook;
5. candidate generation with different contribution functions;
6. factual, anti-AI, duplication, and plain-speech rejection passes;
7. validation of the 25–35 word requirement;
8. final human review and manual use.

The site must say that the workflow does not post, vote, manage accounts, or
replace human review. It must not claim measured time savings, lower error
rates, team adoption, or company ownership without new evidence.

### Client Brands

Do not publish names from the internal protected-brands list. The list is an
operational safety reference, not public evidence of a client relationship.
Use industry categories only. Brand names may be added later only when a public
Vertex source or explicit company authorization confirms that the relationship
may be disclosed.

### Visual Evidence

Use designed, confidentiality-safe artifacts rather than internal screenshots:

- a compact account-portfolio data visualization;
- the drafting and QA workflow diagram;
- a redacted or synthetic example showing how a generic draft becomes a
  community-specific draft;
- explicit evidence labels: `Historical asset`, `My role`, `Strong evidence`,
  and `No automation of posting`.

## About and Education

About should explain the useful intersection of:

- Chinese background and North American study experience;
- Strategic Communication and Business Analytics;
- overseas community operations and content judgment;
- music and event experience;
- evidence-aware, hands-on workflow improvement.

Education is reduced to a concise timeline. It must not occupy more visual
weight than a featured project.

## Visual System

Retain the warm charcoal, bone-white, and amber palette. Retain the Chinese
serif and English monospaced accent pairing, but use readable body typography
and bundle or request deployable webfonts rather than referencing a Windows
filesystem path.

Remove:

- the 100svh hero;
- the oversized ghost character;
- continuous grain animation;
- the marquee;
- glow shadows on dark surfaces;
- equal three-column capability and working-style grids;
- the isolated six-cell metric wall;
- inline `!important` typography overrides;
- universal scroll-reveal treatment.

Allow one signature motion system: a restrained project-media reveal with a
complete reduced-motion fallback. Use lines, spacing, scale, and image contrast
instead of glow to establish hierarchy.

## Navigation and Responsive Behavior

Desktop navigation exposes `Work`, `About`, `Résumé`, `Contact`, and the
language control. Mobile navigation must keep `Work`, `About`, and `Résumé`
reachable through visible links or an accessible menu; it may not remove all
content navigation.

Validate at 320, 375, 414, and 768 pixels as well as 1280 × 720. Requirements:

- no horizontal page scrolling;
- no text or navigation clipped by the viewport;
- project titles and metadata remain visible;
- tap targets are at least 44 pixels where practical;
- project layouts collapse to one column;
- images have useful dimensions and below-the-fold lazy loading;
- use `overflow-x: clip`, not `hidden`, as a final safety rule.

## Accessibility

- Keep semantic landmarks and heading order.
- Preserve visible focus states.
- Keep `aria-pressed` language controls and update document language and
  metadata on language change.
- Provide descriptive alternative text without confidential details.
- Make all project captions available without hover.
- Preserve a comprehensive `prefers-reduced-motion` path.
- Maintain WCAG AA text contrast.

## Testing

Use Node's built-in test runner. Add failing tests before implementation for
each behavioral or structural change.

Automated checks must verify:

1. Vertex Marketing is the first featured case.
2. All displayed Vertex figures match the approved knowledge-base file.
3. The visible historical-asset attribution note exists in both languages.
4. No name from the internal protected-brands list appears in public site copy.
5. The site does not claim team adoption, automatic posting, measured time
   savings, or post-hire growth unsupported by the approved source.
6. The workflow Skill's verified stages and human-review boundary appear.
7. English remains the default and both dictionaries expose identical keys.
8. Track & Traction goals are not presented as completed results.
9. Work appears before education and generic skills.
10. The 100svh hero, marquee, equal capability grid, isolated metric grid,
    Windows font path, inline `!important`, and `Team leAd` typo are absent.
11. Mobile content navigation remains present.
12. Artwork links still open full source files and captions remain visible.

Manual browser QA covers both languages, language persistence, keyboard use,
reduced motion, desktop and required mobile widths, image loading, project
hierarchy, and a clean browser console.

## Implementation Constraints

- Remain a static GitHub Pages site using HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.
- Preserve the public URL.
- Preserve original artwork files.
- Do not introduce a CMS, framework, backend, or analytics dependency as part
  of this redesign.
- Do not add client names, internal screenshots, account identifiers, internal
  policies, colleague details, or other Vertex-confidential material.

## Audit Closure Criteria

The redesign closes the prior Hallmark audit when:

- current positioning replaces the outdated paid-media-first promise;
- Vertex and project evidence appear immediately after the hero;
- every metric has source and attribution context;
- the full-viewport hero is gone;
- repeated equal-card structures are gone;
- motion is reduced to one coherent system;
- glow treatments are removed;
- mobile navigation is complete;
- captions no longer depend on hover;
- product and design documentation match the English-first strategy;
- inline token overrides, overflow masking, local font paths, and copy errors
  are removed.
